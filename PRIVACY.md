# Privacy Policy

**VOICEVOX Speak Selection** processes selected text by sending it to the VOICEVOX Engine running locally on the user's own computer at `http://127.0.0.1:50021`.

## Data handling

- Selected webpage text is sent to the local VOICEVOX Engine only to synthesize speech.
- The extension stores the selected speaker/style ID in Chrome local extension storage.
- The extension does not operate a remote server and does not intentionally transmit selected text to the extension developer.
- The extension does not collect analytics, browsing history, account credentials, or personally identifiable information.

## Third-party software

VOICEVOX Engine and its voice packages are separate third-party software. Their own privacy and licensing terms apply.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, please open a GitHub issue in this repository.
